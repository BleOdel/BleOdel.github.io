Research philosophy

Build. Measure. Understand. Defend.

Four steps, applied to every system I study — from a doctoral VR testbed to a production mixed-reality platform.

01

Understand the system

Identify assumptions, dependencies and attack surfaces.

02

Reproduce the threat

Build controlled experimental environments for meaningful failure modes.

03

Instrument the environment

Collect system and user signals that reveal behavioural change.

04

Engineer the defence

Translate evidence into detection mechanisms, warning systems or architectural improvements.

About

Researcher. Engineer. Builder.

I work at the intersection of cybersecurity research and immersive systems engineering. My background spans computer forensics, cybersecurity, XR development, experimental research, machine learning and applied platform engineering.

My PhD focused on understanding cybersecurity threats in virtual reality environments, but my broader interest is in complex systems where software behaviour, human experience and security intersect.

I enjoy moving between research questions and working systems — building the environments required to investigate a problem, collecting evidence about how those systems behave, and translating those findings into practical engineering solutions.

Education: PhD, Computing and Information Systems, University of Greenwich (2019–2025) · MSc, Computer Forensics and Cyber Security (Distinction), University of Greenwich (2017–2018) · BSc, Computer Science, Ekiti State University, Nigeria (2016).
