Innovate UK KTP · Gener8 Spaces Ltd & Edge Hill University, 2024–Present

Gener8XR: Applied Mixed-Reality Platform Engineering

How do you turn experimental immersive technology into a reusable, maintainable platform?

A production mixed-reality platform unifying room-scale CAVE projection systems with Quest 3-based MR, built as the sole XR Solutions Developer on a 32-month Innovate UK Knowledge Transfer Partnership.

Funded value: £205,332 Stack: Unity · C# · OpenXR · Photon · Quest 3 · Spatial anchors

Mixed-reality platform demo

Captured through the headset in a university clinical skills lab. The room is real, seen through passthrough; everything else is rendered and anchored to it.

Spatial anchoring and persistence

A shared spatial anchoring and persistence system for colocated MR sessions achieves sub-15cm drift detection with automatic realignment, incorporating pose stabilisation and world-anchored scene restoration across users, sessions and devices. That is the core technical challenge in colocated MR: keeping multiple headsets agreeing on where virtual objects sit in physical space.

Bridging Unity and non-technical tools

A bidirectional interoperability layer connects Unity MR scenes to the Intuiface platform using a custom HTTP command API and WebSocket event broadcasting, enabling non-technical users to control live 3D scene content in real time, so Gener8's own staff can operate the platform without touching Unity directly.

Interaction design and reusable engineering

I designed the core interactive interfaces (radial context menus, wrist-mounted controls, and world-anchored panels) for spatial usability and clarity in shared MR environments, informed directly by the security-warning and cognitive-load insights from the human-impact study. A reusable XR development blueprint standardises C# patterns, prefabs and modular architecture, so future projects build on a stable codebase rather than starting from scratch.

Requirements and knowledge transfer

I translated stakeholder discovery and user surveys into a formal Software Requirements Specification (SRS) and Agile user stories, which drove the UX design and development roadmap. I also delivered documentation and internal workshops for non-technical staff on deploying, configuring and operating the platform in educational settings.
